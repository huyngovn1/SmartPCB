SMARTPCB 3 - BAT DAU

1. Giai nen TOAN BO ZIP. Khong chi lay rieng file .pas.
2. Trong Altium: File > Open > SmartPCB_AD2016.PrjScr.
3. Bam tab PCB .PcbDoc can xu ly.
4. DXP > Run Script > PreviewSmart > Run.
   Lenh nay tao hinh truoc/sau de xem thu, chua doi vi tri/duong day PCB.
5. De ap dung tu dong: DXP > Run Script > SmartAuto > Run.
   Tool tao ban sao PCB, tu bo tri + tinh duong day + nhap ket qua.
   Neu chi muon di day, chon SmartRouteOnly thay SmartAuto.

DO RONG DAY: dat trong Design > Rules > Routing > Width truoc khi chay.
Net 3V3: tao rule InNet('3V3'), dat Preferred va uu tien cao hon rule chung.

BAN THU NGHIEM: bo tinh da qua kiem thu Node; adapter CHUA chay thu
trong Altium 16.1. Khong dam bao di du 100%. Xem cac net con lai trong
report.html va chay Tools > Design Rule Check sau khi nhap.
